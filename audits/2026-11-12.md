**Most important fixes:**
1. **The bench still runs on the shared box with a general shell.** `scripts/run-work-order.sh:739-761` strips `GH_TOKEN`, denies `git push` and `gh`, and runs `grok --always-approve --sandbox workspace`, but there is no fresh container (`sandbox-no-secrets`, #221, `factory:blocked`) and the token is still a cached installation token, not one per work order (`per-run-credentials`, #213). This is the largest remaining Best practice gap.
2. **Egress is audit-only everywhere except `gh-factory`.** `gh-factory` is `egress-policy: block` on 8 jobs in 6 workflows; its `release.yml` and `mutation.yml` stay on `audit` (#321). Every workflow in `software-factory`, `line` and `activity-feed` is `audit`, including the `activity-feed` deploy job that holds `CF_API_TOKEN`.
3. **No merge queue, and the MVP is not closed.** No ruleset has a merge-queue rule (#312, `needs-scott`; the queue cannot be enabled on user-owned repos). Together with the two items above, that leaves 6 Best practice rows partial, so #302 (MVP v1.0.0) cannot close.
4. **The monthly Line audit workflow still cannot run itself.** Actions secrets on `software-factory` are `["FACTORY_READ_TOKEN"]` and variables are `[]`, so `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `AUDITOR_*` are unset. The header of `.github/workflows/line-audit.yml` still says "NOT YET RUNNABLE".
